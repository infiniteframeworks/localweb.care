Before we dive in

This questionnaire is for business owners thinking about getting or revamping a website — whether you have a fully-formed vision or just the vague feeling that it's time for change. It's long but you don't need all the answers before you start.

It exists for two reasons: to get your creative juices flowing, and to cut down on back-and-forth during our first call. The more context I have upfront, the sooner we get to the fun part.

  • Take your time. There's no deadline — come back to it whenever you're ready.
  • Progress is saved automatically. Close the tab and pick up right where you left off.
  • Leave any question blank. Partial answers and "I don't know" are genuinely fine — we'll fill in the gaps together.
  • You will be graded. (j/k — but seriously, there are no wrong answers here.)
