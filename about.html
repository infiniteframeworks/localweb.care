The backstory

Hi, I'm Jim.

I have been in the corporate world as a software development manager and director of e-commerce, for longer than I'd like to admit: the kind of roles where you wrangle vendors, lead technical teams, and help SMBs get more out of their digital properties.

But somewhere along the way I started noticing a gap. The small businesses, family-owned shops, neighborhood landscapers, the solo service providers, were getting left behind online. Too small for an agency and they are tired of bugging their nephew who "does websites."

These businesses deserved better.

So I started localweb.care to help small businesses who struggle to compete online. One person, with real experience, helping local businesses level up their web presence.

If you've got a business and a website that isn't working hard enough for you, that's exactly the conversation I want to have.
